🎁 Benefits at a Glance

⚠️ Note: The benefits below apply to full-time employees only. Interns are not eligible for these benefits.

🏖 Paid Leave

Once you've been with us for more than 12 months, you receive 12 paid leave days per year. If you're newer to the team, you earn 1 paid leave day for each month you've worked at the company.

Any unused paid leave days at the end of the year can carry over to the next year, up to a maximum of 5 days.

🏠 Work From Home

You can work from home up to 2 days per month. To keep things balanced, the two days should be at least 2 weeks apart. Of course, exceptions are always understood for emergencies, sick days, or unexpected disruptions.

📊 KPI Bonus

Your bonus is split equally between how the company performs and how your team performs, with each half worth up to 0.5× your monthly salary.

Company KPI (50%)

Tied to the company's annual revenue goal. If we hit 100% of the goal, you earn 0.5× your monthly salary.

Team KPI (50%)

Tied to your team's KPI achievement. If your team reaches 120%, you earn 0.6× your monthly salary.

Example: Company at 100% + team at 120% = 1.1× monthly salary bonus for that period.
Extra: For every 300,000 USD above the revenue goal, an extra 0.5 is added to the KPI multiplier, so going above and beyond is always rewarded.
🏥 Health Insurance

Health insurance is entirely optional. Your salary is the same regardless of whether you choose to enroll or not. No pressure either way.

🛡 Voluntary Social Security (BHXH TN)

If you choose to enroll, the company covers 75% of the contribution and your share is 25%, which is deducted directly from your salary. If you opt out, your salary stays exactly as it is.

🚗 Parking Allowance

If you commute to the office without a bike, you'll receive a monthly parking allowance of 50,000 VND. If you ride a bike, this allowance doesn't apply.

Daily Tasks

Check In & Check Out

Open TimeStation at the start of your workday and tap Check In. At the end of the day, tap Check Out. Do this every day. This is how attendance is tracked.

Don't have an account yet? Ask Ms. Ha to create one for you.

Post Your Daily To-Do List

At the start of each day, post a short list of what you plan to work on in the #daily-updates Slack channel. Just a few bullet points to keep the whole team aligned.
