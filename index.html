Full-time team members: The benefits below apply to full-time employees. Interns are not eligible.

Benefits at a glance

🏖

Paid leave

After 12 months, receive 12 paid leave days per year. Before that, earn one day for each month worked. Up to five unused days may carry over.

🏠

Work from home

Work from home up to two days per month, ideally at least two weeks apart. Emergencies and unexpected disruptions are handled with flexibility.

📊

KPI bonus

Your bonus combines company performance and team performance. Company and team achievement each contribute half of the standard multiplier.

🏥

Health insurance

Health insurance is optional. Your salary remains the same whether or not you choose to enroll.

🛡

Voluntary social security

If you enroll, the company covers 75% and your 25% share is deducted from salary. Opting out does not change your salary.

🚗

Parking allowance

Employees who commute to the office without a bike receive a monthly parking allowance of 50,000 VND.

Your daily rhythm

A short, reliable routine for attendance, time off, and team communication.

1

Check in and out with TimeStation

Open TimeStation at the start of your workday and tap Check In. At the end of the day, tap Check Out. Do this every day — it's how attendance is tracked.

Need an account? Ask Ms. Ha to create one for you.
2

Day off or working from home

Day off: Send a private message to Ms. Tho, Ms. Ha, or Mr. Duc, then post in #attendance-teamvn to log it for the team.

Working from home: Post in #attendance-teamvn to let the team know.

⏰ For longer vacations, please give advance notice:
Taking more than 3 days off → request at least 7 days in advance
Taking more than 7 days off → request at least 2 weeks in advance
